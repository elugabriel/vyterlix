Common questions.

Do I need to be good with numbers?

No. Every figure comes with a one-line explanation and a suggested next step.

What if I don't use any of those tools?

Upload a spreadsheet or type your figures in. Plenty of businesses start that way and connect a system later.

Who can see my business figures?

Only you and the people you invite. You choose who joins and what each person can do.

Is my data secure?

Connections are encrypted, your access keys are stored encrypted, and your data is held on servers in Europe. The details are in our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

There are plans for small and mid-sized businesses and for larger organisations. You see the price in the app before you pay, and VAT is added at checkout. Need something tailored? Contact us.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any phone browser as well as on a computer.
