Running a business is demanding enough without searching for the numbers.

Sales sit in one system, money in another and customers in a third. By the time everything is brought together, it is already out of date.

Scattered figures

Your till, accounts, advertising and messages each tell part of the story, but no one sees the whole picture.

Problems found too late

A slow month, a late-paying client or a fall in repeat customers is easy to miss until it starts to hurt.

Reports that go unread

Charts and tables do not tell you what to do on Monday morning. A clear recommendation does.

What Vyterlix does

From scattered figures to a clear plan, in five steps.

Every feature below is available today and works from your own figures.

1

Understand

A single page, Today, shows where you stand. Your key figures, a simple health score and ready-made reports sit behind it.

2

What changed shows why a figure moved, in clear language. You can also ask questions: Ask Vyterlix answers from your own business data.

3

Predict

The forecast shows where sales, bookings and stock are heading, so you can plan staffing, orders and cash flow.

4

Alerts warn you early, and each one comes with practical steps you can take.

5

Turn recommendations into actions with an owner. Vyterlix remembers what you tried and what it learned, so future suggestions improve.

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to be good with numbers or technology?

No. Everything is written in clear, everyday language, and the app explains what each figure means and what to do about it.

What if I do not use any of those systems?

You can upload a spreadsheet or enter your figures by hand. Many businesses start that way and connect a system later.

Who can see my business figures?

Only you and the people you invite. You decide who joins and what they can do.

Is my information secure?

Connections to your other systems are protected, the keys you provide are stored encrypted, and your data is held on servers in Europe. Read more in our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

There are plans for small and medium-sized businesses and for larger organisations. Prices are shown inside the app before you pay, and VAT is added at checkout. If you need a tailored plan, please contact us.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Vyterlix works in your phone's browser as well as on a computer.
