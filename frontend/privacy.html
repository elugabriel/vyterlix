Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Dolaris Limited ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use Vyterlix, our business intelligence service, through our website and mobile app. We are the controller of the personal information described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a password, which we store only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Business details you provide: your business name and profile, the people you invite, and the settings you choose.
  • Business data you add or connect: figures such as sales, expenses, bookings, stock, invoices and customer records, entered by hand, uploaded from files, or brought in from systems you choose to connect (for example accounting, shop, payment, customer or marketing systems).
  • Connection keys: when you connect another system, the key or token it gives us is stored encrypted and used only to read the data you have authorised.
  • Messaging information: if you connect WhatsApp Business, we record that a message was sent or received and when. We do not store the message text or the contact's phone number; the contact is kept only as a scrambled (hashed) identifier.
  • Usage and security information: sign-in events, device and browser type, and a record of important actions, which we keep to protect accounts and investigate problems.

2. How we use it

  • To provide Vyterlix: calculating your figures, explaining changes, forecasting, and producing alerts and recommendations.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix faults.
  • To send service messages, such as email verification, password reset and important notices.
  • To manage plans and billing.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.

We rely on these lawful bases: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running and securing the service, and complying with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your business data to advertise to you.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with organisations that help us run the service, and only as needed:

  • our hosting and email providers;
  • our payment provider, when you buy a plan;
  • the systems you choose to connect, in the way those connections require;
  • professional advisers, regulators or the authorities where the law requires.

Only you and the people you invite to your business can see your business figures.

4. Where your data is held

Your data is held on servers in Europe. Where any provider processes information outside the UK or the European Economic Area, we take steps to make sure it is protected to an appropriate standard.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long afterwards as is needed for legal, accounting and security reasons. You can ask us to delete your account and business data at any time, subject to those obligations.

6. Cookies and similar storage

Vyterlix uses only the storage that is necessary to keep you signed in and remember your choices. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. Your rights

Under data protection law you may ask us to give you a copy of your personal information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. To make a request, contact us using the details below. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections, encrypted storage of connection keys, access controls, and a tamper-resistant record of important actions. No system is completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

9. Children

Vyterlix is a business service and is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will tell you about significant changes.
